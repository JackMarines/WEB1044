WHY TRAVEL TO VINH HY

A hidden paradise of turquoise bays, coral reefs, and a tranquil village.

Find the quieter side of Vietnam’s coast. Spend the morning on the water, explore a hidden beach, and leave room for a long lunch by the bay.

Untouched natural beauty

Cliffs, clear water, and quiet coves make the shoreline worth taking slowly.

Breathtaking coral reefs

Slip beneath the surface and see a colourful side of the bay.

Hidden beaches

Find a sandy patch to swim, rest, and enjoy the salt air.

Fresh local cuisine

Seafood, simple cooking, and a place to sit a little longer.
